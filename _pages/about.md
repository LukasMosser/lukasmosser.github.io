## Hey There, I'm Lukas!

I'm an advanced data scientist (lead-equivalent) at [Aker BP](https://akerbp.com) in Norway.
There I'm the value stream manager responsible for generative AI in subsurface applications,
and I lead the Aker BP data science community of practice.

### What I'm Working On
Most of my recent work is about making large language and vision-language models useful for geoscience:
- **Conversational access to exploration knowledge.** [Exploration Robot Chat](https://doi.org/10.2118/218439-ms) uses LLMs to surface decades of exploration reports and data (SPE Norway Subsurface Conference 2024).
- **Teaching models to reason about geology.** [Geo-Strat-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25000) is a synthetic environment with an executable verifier for training vision-language models to reconstruct geological event histories using reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.
- **Generative models for subsurface modelling.** This ranges from [deep diffusion models for facies modelling](https://doi.org/10.3997/2214-4609.2023101627) back to the GAN-based work from my PhD.

I'm also interested in the less glamorous part: putting ML models into production, building good APIs for them,
and making ML-based software pleasant to use.

### Previous Experience
Before Aker BP, I was Principal Geo / Data Scientist and R&D team lead at Earth Science Analytics,
a geoscience AI startup. There we worked on Bayesian deep learning and uncertainty quantification for seismic interpretation,
including our [Geophysics paper on calibration of Bayesian CNNs](https://doi.org/10.1190/geo2021-0318.1).

Before that, I did my PhD at Imperial College London (ICL) under the supervision of Olivier Dubrule and Martin Blunt.
My main focus was on combining deep learning with Bayesian inversion techniques to solve high-dimensional inverse problems.
My PhD thesis is publicly available [here](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/handle/10044/1/80165) in Imperial's archives.

### Teaching Experience
During my time at Imperial College, I co-created the Machine Learning Module 
for the [MSc. in Applied Computational Sciences and Engineering](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/pg/earth-science/computational-science/).
I created the material for the practical sessions and taught them to more than 60 students.

You can see a video about the course [here on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m9a0YU2IdUU).

### Technical Interests
Bayesian methods for inverse problems, Bayesian deep learning, (deep) generative models, geological modelling,
and, more recently, LLMs, vision-language models and reinforcement learning for geoscience reasoning.

### Community Outreach
I serve as a committee member on the [EAGE committee for AI](https://eage.org/communities/artificial-intelligence-community/),
where I help organize weekly updates on All-Things-AI, conferences, workshops, and dedicated sessions.
In 2024 I received the EAGE Arie van Weelden Award.

I regularly review publications in the domain of stochastic image reconstruction, 
deep generative models, and machine-learning-based applications for geophysics.

Open-Source Projects
------
A few code projects I've worked on:
- [Neural-Rock-Typing](https://github.com/lukasmosser/neural_rock_typing): Model interpretability viewer and API for carbonate rock types.
- [ASI-Pytorch](https://github.com/lukasmosser/asi-pytorch): Collection of automated seismic interpretation models written in PyTorch. PRs welcome!
- [PorousMediaGAN](https://github.com/LukasMosser/PorousMediaGan): Accompanying repository for our paper on porous media reconstruction in [Phys. Rev. E](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.96.043309).
- [SNIST](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6622822): A benchmark for seismic velocity inversion from synthetics.
